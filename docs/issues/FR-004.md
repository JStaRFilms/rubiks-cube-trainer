# FR-004: Local history and statistics

## User story
Review saved sessions and progress without treating failures as successful times or combining unlike drills.

## Proposed flow
Save stopped attempts by trainer/session with configuration and durations. Show recent attempts, best successful time, mean, median, standard deviation and applicable trimmed ao5/ao12. Filter by plan-defined dimensions and show counts.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §3.4](../imports/PLAN.md). Separate execution and preparation trends. Keep case reps, set runs and Cross attempts distinct. Record actual versus selected difficulty metadata and distinguish generated witnesses from optional self-reports.

## Dependencies
B01 local records and B03 statistics are planned; trainer tasks provide metadata. Requires FR-003 and FR-005.

## Acceptance criteria
- Records survive restart.
- DNF is never zero or silently omitted from a purported competition-style average; +2 and DNF policy is explicit.
- Comparisons identify compatible settings and counts; incompatible tasks do not share averages.
