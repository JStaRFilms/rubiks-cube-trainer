# FR-006: Cross trainer

## User story
Practice planning and executing only a cross on the physical cube.

## Proposed flow
Choose color and maximum optimal HTM length K from 1–8. Present a verified nontrivial challenge, time the attempt, then reveal an optimal cross solution in notation and 3D.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §4.1](../imports/PLAN.md). K is a ceiling, not exact depth. Record actual depth. Generation and reveal paths are distinct; verify resulting state rather than trusting construction labels.

## Dependencies
B04 after shared foundation B01–B03, then Q01, are planned. Requires FR-002/003/004.

## Acceptance criteria
- Generated states are legal, nontrivial and independently verified at actual optimal depth 1..K for every supported color.
- Reveal solves the selected cross with verified optimal length and correct frame/color.
- Persist selected ceiling, actual depth and color; do not claim physical completion is observed.
