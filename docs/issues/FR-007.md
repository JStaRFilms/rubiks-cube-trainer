# FR-007: Cross+1 trainer

## User story
Plan cross and first pair together, then execute until cross and at least one pair are solved.

## Proposed flow
Choose cross ceiling K, combined witness cap L and any-pair (default) or FR/FL/BR/BL target. Generate with bounded worker search, retain a witness, hide its slot during any-pair attempt, then review the found solution.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §4.2](../imports/PLAN.md). L is a witnessed upper bound, not optimality. K constrains independent cross depth, not a required optimal prefix. Any-pair and selected-slot goals need explicit checks.

## Dependencies
B05 generation proof precedes B06 integration; Q02 follows. These are planned tasks. Requires FR-002/003/004/006/012.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every challenge meets cross depth <=K and has a full-state-verified cross-plus-permitted-pair witness <=L.
- Targeted mode proves the selected slot; any-pair considers permitted goals.
- Exhaustion/cancellation gives retry or error, never a weaker challenge.
- Generated witness metadata and optional user-reported executed slot remain distinct.
