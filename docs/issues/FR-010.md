# FR-010: ZBLL trainer

## User story
Practice standard ZBLL cases by family or subset in recognition or execution mode.

## Proposed flow
Freeze inventory convention, then choose family/cases, angle/AUF options, mode and algorithm. Start each setup aligned and solved outside the intended last-layer case; reveal identity and solution after attempt.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §4.5](../imports/PLAN.md). Target standard 493-case convention with explicit family, AUF, PLL inclusion and solved-state policy. Preserve solved F2L and oriented LL edges. Do not advertise complete coverage from preview seeds.

## Dependencies
B10 inventory/convention gate, B11 trainer and Q04 are planned. Requires FR-002/003/004/012.

## Acceptance criteria
- A coverage manifest substantiates any complete-trainer claim and every case has validated identity, setup and solution.
- Recognition does not leak identity; personal overrides solve the same case.
- Progress shows attempted/available counts and distinguishes variants under the declared convention.
