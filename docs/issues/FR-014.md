# FR-014: Video reconstruction research

## User story
Explore local reconstruction of moves and timing from uploaded solve video and known scramble, with uncertainty and correction.

## Proposed flow
After state/notation contracts stabilize, run a separately authorized bounded experiment using consented samples and independent ground truth. Report move error, exact sequence and final state separately, timing, correction effort, abstentions and resource/device limits. Integrate only after evidence review and owner go.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §8](../imports/PLAN.md). Preserve detected versus corrected moves; do not invent moves through ambiguous spans. No server fallback or upload. Numerical release thresholds derive from measured baseline.

## Dependencies
R01 requires B02, an approved packet, authorized isolated worktree and consented samples; R02 reviews. Later integration is conditional. Research is independent of trainer delivery.

## Acceptance criteria
- Packet and stop criteria approved before research starts.
- Evidence states normalization, ground truth, timing uncertainty, failure/abstention, runtime/memory/download and correction effort.
- Results distinguish exact sequence reconstruction from correct final state.
- No integration or performance claim before owner reviews evidence and authorizes go.
