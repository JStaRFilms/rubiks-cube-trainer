# FR-015: Sourced teaching modules

## User story
Learn specific training skills through concise instruction tied to a compatible practice activity.

## Proposed flow
After practice activities exist, research permitted sources and record attribution and disagreements. Draft objective, prerequisites, example, practice activity and understanding check. Owner edits/approves; pilot with intended skill level.

## Technical considerations
Follow [PLAN.md §9.1](../imports/PLAN.md). Do not scrape or republish material without rights. No automatic coaching or full course system is implied.

## Dependencies
T01 is planned after Q03 with separate research authorization and owner editorial gate. Teaching is not a core trainer dependency.

## Acceptance criteria
- Published module has permitted, traceable sources and owner approval.
- Module includes objective, prerequisites, example, compatible practice and check.
- Pilot feedback is recorded before expanding the teaching track.
